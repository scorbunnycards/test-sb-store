About Us

Scorbunny Cards started as a small, hands-on passion project: taking the Pokémon cards we love and turning them into full display pieces, one custom extension at a time. What began as a personal hobby has grown into a small independent shop serving collectors who want their favorite cards to feel like art, not just cardboard in a sleeve.

Every extension is individually designed around the card it's made for — matching colors, lighting, and mood so the printed background feels like a natural continuation of the artwork. Pieces are made to order in small batches, checked by hand, and packed carefully before they leave us.

We're a small operation, which means every order gets personal attention — but it also means production takes real time. Thank you for supporting an independent, collector-run shop.

Scorbunny Cards is an independent, fan-run shop and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Nintendo, Game Freak, Creatures Inc., or The Pokémon Company. Pokémon and all related names are trademarks of their respective owners. Our products are unofficial, made-to-order fan accessories intended for personal collector use.

Shipping & Delivery

Because every extension is made to order, please allow a short production window before your order ships. Once your piece is ready, it's carefully packaged to prevent damage in transit and shipped with a tracked delivery service.

Production Time

Most orders are produced and ready to ship within a few business days of payment being confirmed. Larger or more detailed pieces (such as multi-card A4 extensions) may take a little longer — if there's any delay we'll let you know by email.

Preparation & Dispatch Days

Orders are prepared over the weekend and dispatched Monday through Friday. Orders placed later in the week may ship on the following business day — this is normal and doesn't mean anything has gone wrong with your order.

Shipping Time & Cost

A flat shipping fee is shown at checkout before you confirm your order. Delivery times vary depending on your location and the courier's own transit times, and a tracking number is provided once your order is on its way.

Returns / Withdrawal Policy

Please read before ordering

We want you to be happy with your extension. Because every piece is custom-made to your chosen card, frame color, case, and specifications, please read the following carefully before placing your order.

Custom / Personalized Orders

Under EU consumer protection law, the standard 14-day right of withdrawal does not apply to goods made to the consumer's specifications or clearly personalized (Directive 2011/83/EU, Art. 16(c)). As every extension we make is produced specifically for your chosen card and options, orders generally cannot be cancelled or returned simply for a change of mind once production has begun.

If you need to cancel or change an order, contact us as soon as possible — if production hasn't started yet, we'll do our best to accommodate you.

Damaged, Defective, or Incorrect Items

If your order arrives damaged, defective, or different from what you ordered, please contact us within 14 days of delivery at scorbunnycards@gmail.com with your order number and clear photos of the issue. We'll arrange a replacement, repair, or refund as appropriate, free of charge.

Lost in Transit

If tracking shows your order as lost or significantly delayed beyond the expected delivery window, let us know and we'll investigate with the courier and find a fair resolution.

This section is provided for general information and reflects our standard policy — it does not override any mandatory statutory rights you may have under the consumer protection laws of your country of residence.
